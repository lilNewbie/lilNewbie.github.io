~/about
// who, what, why

I'm a graduate researcher in the MS Computer Science program at NYU, finishing May 2027. Most of my work sits at the intersection of machine learning and physical signals; medical imaging, audio, biosignals, materials data, where careful preprocessing matters as much as architecture and the cost of being wrong is real.

Day-to-day right now: orchestrating Slurm pipelines for 3D CNNs on ~60 TB of breast MRI at NYU Langone, working toward five-year cancer risk forecasting. Before that I spent nine months at NIE / NTU Singapore building Conformer-based EEG and eye-gaze models for affect detection (~90% accuracy on a held-out test set), and a stretch at Samsung SEED Lab on score-based generative models for speech denoising.

Outside the lab: I write Bayesian causal models, build agentic RAG systems, run distributed pipelines over global trade data, and backtest systematic trading strategies. I also TA'd ML for ~70 students at VIT Chennai. Always interested in talking to people working on hard problems.

quant { }

Regime-Filtered TSMOM

Pre-registered backtest of a frequency-domain regime filter for time-series momentum on a 10-ETF universe (2010–2026). Built a rolling Welch PSD log-ratio classifier, showed it's empirically independent of a vol filter (mask agreement ~50%), and decomposed the joint regime space into 4 cells. Intersection of both filters → Sharpe 0.60, stable across in-sample / OOS / sub-period (range 0.51–0.66).
